## Brand & Style

This design system serves an intelligent pedestrian walking tour and location-aware audio guide. The experience bridges physical navigation and immersive storytelling. The brand personality is grounded, observant, and quietly authoritative—like a well-crafted field journal fused with modern geospatial instrumentation. 

The aesthetic blends **Modern Cartographic Functionalism** with **Tactile Wayfinding Elements**:
- **Clarity over ornament:** UI chrome remains unobtrusive against active maps and ambient cityscapes while remaining readable under direct outdoor sunlight.
- **Physicality and feedback:** Route handles, audio scrubbers, and bottom sheet sheets have tangible weight, precise friction, and high touch target precision.
- **Wayfinding cues:** Vibrant emerald pulses signify active navigation vectors and forward momentum, anchored by deep slate topography and warm architectural terracotta milestones.

## Colors

The palette balances historical earthiness with modern vector cartography:
- **Primary (`#E06D53` - Terracotta):** Used for focal points of interest (POIs), playback controls, primary action triggers, and story milestones. It evokes brick lanes, baked clay tiles, and urban heritage.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` - Deep Emerald / Teal):** Reserved strictly for active wayfinding states: walking trail vectors, live GPS pulses, progress halos, and audio active-sync indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Warm Amber):** Denotes secondary highlights, architectural commentary, curated detours, and contextual tags.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#1E293B` Slate & `#F8FAFC` Warm Chalk):** `#1E293B` provides high-contrast legibility for route text and dark-surface map overlays. The base ground uses `#F8FAFC` with surface containers nested in `#F1F5F9` and `#E2E8F0` to mimic heavy stone-stock paper.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes outdoor glanceability and rapid orientation:
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides open counters and sturdy terminals that maintain clarity when an iPhone or Android screen jostles during walking.
- Headings use negative letter-spacing for a composed, modern editorial posture.
- Small indicators, category chips, and distance markers rely on uppercase `label-sm` with widened tracking (+0.06em) for instant identification.
- Time codes, scrub timestamps, and remaining walking durations leverage `mono-timer` (Space Mono) to prevent layout shifts as seconds elapse.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for single-handed mobile navigation over an interactive map view:
- **Bottom-Anchored Architecture:** Primary controls, sheet drawers, playback scrubbing, and tour stops are weighted within the lower 60% of the viewport (the thumb zone).
- **Responsive Fluid Overlay:** Mobile viewports use a 4-column layout with 16px outer margins, allowing bottom modal sheets to hug device edges. Tablets expand into a split screen (8 columns): persistent map on the right, audio guide narrative deck on the left. Desktop administration/planning layouts scale to a 12-column grid.
- **Component Breathing Room:** Touch targets for track scrubber handles, GPS re-center FABs, and detour selection badges adhere to minimum 48x48px boundaries with `space-md` (12px) separation to avoid mis-taps during movement.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Frosted Cartographic Stratification** with ambient, tinted drop-shadows:
- **Ground Layer (Elevation 0):** Full-bleed vector map tiles and topography lines.
- **Floating Controls (Elevation 1):** Map controls (compass, re-center, zoom) and floating search badges use a semi-opaque background (`rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.92)` in light mode, `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.92)` in night mode) with an 8px backdrop blur and a fine border outline (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`). Shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06)`.
- **Tour Narrative Cards & Bottom Sheets (Elevation 2):** Elevated interactive modals feature smooth directional elevation: `0 -4px 20px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`.
- **Active Modals & Persistent Player Dock (Elevation 3):** Fixed audio controls float above bottom sheets with an amber/terracotta tinted shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(224, 109, 83, 0.18)` to signify active acoustic state.

## Shapes

The shape system pairs structured cards with ergonomic organic pills:
- **Base Surfaces & Cards:** Standard cards and bottom-sheet containers use Level 2 roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px` up to `1rem` / `16px` for outer containers). Sheet top handles feature softened edges for clear drag affording.
- **Pill Elements:** Filter tags, playback time chips, walking pace triggers, and turn-by-turn instruction badges use complete pill radiuses (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create distinction between spatial content containers and quick touch capsules.

## Components

### 1. Primary & Audio Buttons
- **Play/Pause Floating Beacon:** 56x56px circular button with primary terracotta background (`#E06D53`), white glyph, and a soft pulsing emerald perimeter ring (`#0D9488`) when narrative is actively tracking movement.
- **Secondary Actions:** White or slate background with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), Level 2 roundedness, active state shifting to warm stone (`#F1F5F9`).

### 2. Audio Scrubber Controls
- **Track Rail:** A 4px neutral rail (`#E2E8F0`) topped with a dual-color fill: traversed segment in Terracotta (`#E06D53`), buffered geographical sync segment in Emerald (`#0D9488`).
- **Scrubber Thumb:** A 16x16px tactile circle with an outer 2px white rim and drop shadow. Expands to 20x20px on drag.
- **Waveform Markers:** Integrated mini milestone notches on the scrubber timeline indicating geofenced POI markers ahead.

### 3. Tactile Drag Reorder Handles
- Tour sequence stops contain a 24x24px reorder handle consisting of 6 debossed dots (two vertical columns of three dots in `#94A3B8`). On touch-and-hold, the parent card scales up to 102% scale with enhanced ambient shadow.

### 4. Crisp Pill Badges
- **Status / Distance Badges:** Full-pill shape with `label-sm` text. Distance to next stop displays in `#0D9488` tint (`rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.12)` background, `#0D9488` solid text).
- **Tour Category Tags:** Terracotta and Amber tinted pills indicating narrative genre (e.g., "Architecture", "True Crime", "Local Flora").

### 5. Wayfinding Path & Map Overlays
- **Trail Vector:** 6px solid Emerald stroke (`#0D9488`) with rounded caps and a faint outer glow (`rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.25)`).
- **Completed Path:** Segment transitions to a refined slate dash (`#94A3B8`) once audio narration for that zone is logged.
- **Compass & Turn Pill:** Top-center mounted pill container displaying upcoming walking cue ("Turn right onto St. Anne St. in 40m") in high-contrast `#1E293B` slate.

### 6. Interactive Tour Cards
- Surfaces feature clean 16px corner radiuses, nested padding (`space-lg`), and an edge-to-edge mini thumbnail preview with duration badge overlay in `mono-timer` typography.
